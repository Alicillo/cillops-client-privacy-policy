# Cillops Client — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Cillops Client ("the client") is a Minecraft mod. This policy explains what the client and its Discord application do with your data.

## What the client collects

The client does not collect, store or sell personal data on any server of ours.

- **Discord Rich Presence:** while the game runs, the client tells the Discord app on your own computer to show "Cillops Client" and how long you have been playing. This goes only to your local Discord app, through Discord's own connection. We do not receive it.
- **Settings:** your module settings, key binds and HUD layout are saved only in files on your own computer, in the Minecraft `config` folder.
- **Minecraft account:** the client never reads, stores or sends your Minecraft or Microsoft login details.

## Third parties

Discord shows your presence under Discord's own privacy policy: https://discord.com/privacy

## Children

The client is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
